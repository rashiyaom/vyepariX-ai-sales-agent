AI Sales Agent Platform
Overview
The AI Sales Intelligence & Voice Agent Platform is an AI-powered sales automation
platform that helps businesses discover, qualify, enrich, and engage high-quality
prospects from multiple public sources. Instead of manually searching for customer
requirements across different platforms, businesses can use one centralized platform to
identify opportunities, enrich prospect information, automate outreach, and accelerate
sales. The platform combines AI Lead Discovery, Market Intelligence, Lead
Enrichment, AI Voice Automation, CRM Integration, and Sales Analytics into a single
enterprise solution, reducing manual prospecting while improving sales productivity.

User Journey
Step Workflow
1. The client registers on the AI Sales Agent Platform through the web or mobile
application and starts a free trial.
2. The client provides their company website, company details, business documents,
products, and services to help the AI understand their business.
3. The AI researches the client's business, identifies where potential customers
publicly post their requirements, and displays relevant opportunities with available
prospect details.
4. The client reviews the discovered opportunities and subscribes to Lead Generation,
AI Calling, or both based on their business needs.
5. For AI Calling, the client either configures their own calling infrastructure or uses
the platform's built-in calling service and provides product or service information
for AI-powered conversations.
6. The system validates the products and services to ensure they are suitable for AI
selling. If validation is inconclusive, the request is forwarded to an administrator for
approval.
7. The client selects target industries, locations, keywords, or global search criteria
to discover relevant prospects.
8. The AI continuously discovers public requirements shared by prospects, enriches
prospects with company information, available contact details, and links to the
original requirement posts.
9. The client searches, filters, analyzes, exports discovered leads, or uploads their
own lead list for AI Voice campaigns.
10. The client schedules campaigns based on the prospect's location or time zone, or
launches them immediately.
11. The AI Voice Agent conducts multilingual outbound conversations, qualifies
prospects, answers FAQs, schedules callbacks (till the call is not received by the
prospect), leaves voicemails, retries unanswered calls, and automatically
generates call transcripts, summaries, and next-best action recommendations.
Once a prospect responds, all further communication is handled by the client.
12. Interested prospects are automatically highlighted, allowing the client to prioritize
follow-ups. The client can monitor campaign performance, review AI insights, and
repeat campaigns daily, weekly, or monthly as required.
Expected Outcomes
1. Discover high-intent prospects from multiple public sources.
2. AI-powered lead enrichment, scoring, and qualification.
3. Automated multilingual voice outreach.
4. Reduced manual prospecting, qualification, and sales efforts.
5. Higher sales productivity, conversion rates, and pipeline visibility.
6. Increased meetings, customer engagement, and revenue opportunities.
7. Real-time market intelligence for better sales targeting and decision-making.
Important Notes
1. Every application should be multilingual and support all major languages,
allowing users to access and use the platform in their preferred language for a
seamless and inclusive experience.
2. Leads are discovered only from publicly available sources or supported platform
integrations.
3. Contact information (email and phone number) depends on public availability.
4. If contact information is unavailable, users can connect with prospects
through the original source platform where the requirement was published.
5. The platform always displays the original requirement post URL and source
platform for complete transparency.
Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
Unlike traditional lead generation tools, the AI Sales Agent Platform not only
discovers high-intent prospects from multiple public sources, but also qualifies
who is actually looking for the product/service, enriches, engages, and nurtures
them through AI-powered multilingual voice conversations, delivering an end-to-
end autonomous sales workflow from lead discovery to conversion within a single
unified platform.
